# System Usability Scale (SUS)

The standard ten statements by John Brooke (1996, "SUS: a quick and dirty
usability scale"). Give it on paper straight after the tasks, before the
closing questions. "The system" means the SeatPlan website.

Circle one number for each statement.
1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

| 9 | I felt very confident using the system. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

If the tester skips a statement, ask them to circle 3.

## How to score (done by the team, not the tester)

1. Odd statements (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): score = answer minus 1.
2. Even statements (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): score = 5 minus answer.
3. Add the ten scores (0 to 40).
4. Multiply by 2.5. The SUS score is between 0 and 100.

A SUS score is not a percentage. Compare scores between our own testers and
versions; do not compare one tester's score with another product.
