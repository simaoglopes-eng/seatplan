# Consent note

**Study:** usability test of SeatPlan, a student project for Introduction to
Programming at Nova SBE.

**What you will do:** try a few tasks on a website for about 30 minutes,
think aloud, and fill in a short questionnaire.

**What we write down:** what you do on the screen, what you say, how long each
task takes, and your questionnaire answers. We do **not** record video or
audio unless you tick the box below.

**What we do not collect:** your real name in the results, your email, your
phone number. Inside the website you only use invented names and addresses
ending in `@example.com`. Please do not type your own details.

**How we use it:** only to improve SeatPlan and to write our course report.
Results are shown with a code (for example P3), never your name. Notes are
kept by the team and deleted after the course ends.

**Your rights:** taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without
giving a reason. You can ask us to delete your notes until the report is
handed in.
