# SeatPlan for guests

You received a personal link from the organiser, for example
`https://.../e/AbC...`. It is your key: anyone with it can choose a seat for
you, so do not forward it. If you lose it, ask the organiser for a new one; the
old link then stops working.

## 1. Open your page

The page shows the event, where it takes place (the venue name), the room
and every seat. Each seat has a state:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Free | You can pick it |
| Held | Someone is picking it right now (held for up to 5 minutes) |
| Taken | Someone sits there |
| Friend | Someone you marked as "I know them" sits there |
| Mine | Your seat |
| Blocked | Not available to you (blocked, or reserved for other guests) |

Seats marked accessible are step-free.

## 2. Say whether you are coming

Answer yes, no or not sure yet. Answering "no" gives your seat back, so
someone else can have it.

## 3. Tell us who you know (optional)

Search for people by the name you can see and mark the ones you know. Only you
see this list. It is used for two things: their seat shows as "Friend" on your
map, and the recommendations try to put you near them.

## 4. Pick a seat

- **Recommendations** list the best free seats for you, best first, with
  short reasons such as "Next to 2 people you know". They only use your own
  list and your group: whether someone else listed you never changes them, so
  they cannot tell you who did.
- Click a seat to **hold** it. Nobody else can take it for 5 minutes.
- Click **Confirm** to make it yours. You get a confirmation email.

If someone else was faster, you see "Seat ... was just taken by someone else":
pick another seat; the map is already up to date.

## 5. Change or give back your seat

Pick another seat and confirm it: your old seat is released at the same moment
and you get an email about the change. "Give back my seat" releases it and
sends a short cancellation email.

After the organiser's change deadline these buttons are switched off. Contact
the organiser if something changes after that.

## 6. Your privacy

The organiser chooses how names appear to other guests: full names, first
name and last initial ("Ana S."), or no names at all ("Guest"). You can always
choose to appear as "Guest", whatever the event setting. Other guests never see
your email, your dietary note or whether you need an accessible seat.
