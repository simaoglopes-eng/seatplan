# Putting your restaurant on SeatPlan: guide for owners

This guide takes you from a new account to a published restaurant that diners can find and book.
It follows the six steps of the setup: identity, location, photos, opening hours, rooms, and review and publish.
You can stop after any step and come back later: the restaurant stays a **draft** (only you can see it) until you publish.

## 1. Create your account

Open **For restaurant owners** in the header, then **create an owner account**. Give your name, email and a long password, tick the
consent box and ask for a code. We send a six-digit code to your email; type it to finish. The code lasts 15 minutes and
allows five tries. After that you land on **My restaurants**, which lists only your own restaurants, each with a card showing
whether it is a draft or published and what is still missing.

## 2. Identity and location

**Add a restaurant** starts a short form.

- **Name** (required), a **short description** (up to 300 characters), one to three **cuisines**, a **price range** (€ to €€€€,
  per person: under 15 €, 15-30 €, 30-60 €, over 60 €), and optionally a phone, website, Instagram and a Google Maps link.
- The restaurant's address on the web is made from its name (`/restaurants/your-name`). You can change it while the restaurant
  is a draft; once published it stays fixed so links people shared keep working.
- **Location:** type the address and tap the map to place the pin, then drag the pin to fine-tune it. **Use my current
  location** asks your browser only when you press it. If the map does not load, you can type the latitude and longitude instead.
  The pin matters: it is what puts you on the search map, makes "near me" work, and lets the app work out where the sun is.

## 3. Photos

Add up to ten photos of the restaurant. Pick one as the **cover** (the first photo diners see), put them in order, and write a
short description of each ("The dining room by the river"): it is read aloud to people who cannot see the picture. Photos are
resized in your browser before upload (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 800 KB after resizing). A restaurant with no photo can be
published, but the review page recommends adding one. While the restaurant is a draft its photos are private to you.

## 4. Opening hours

If you have no hours yet, SeatPlan offers a model to start from: *Lunch 12:00-15:00 and Dinner 19:00-23:00, Tuesday to Sunday*.
Nothing is saved until you press **Use this**, and you can change anything afterwards: services per day, how long a party of each
size stays (the turn times), the number of guests who may arrive per seating, and the days you are closed.

## 5. Rooms: draw your restaurant

This is the heart of the setup. The room editor works with a mouse and on a phone. On a computer the tools are on the left and
the properties of whatever you select are on the right; on a phone the tools are at the bottom and the properties slide up as a sheet.
**Save** is at the top, with **Undo** and **Redo**. If you close the tab by accident, your unsaved work comes back next time. If
a second tab saved before you, SeatPlan tells you and keeps your work so you can decide.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | Draw the walls of a room: straight sides, curved sides, L-shapes. Drag a corner to move it, drag the middle of a side to add a corner or bend it. |
| **Walls** | Add **windows**, **doors** and **glass walls** to a side. Mark the **main entrance**. For a window you can write what the view is ("The Tagus river"), what it looks like at night, add a photo of the view, and say when it gets sun. |
| **North** | Set which way north points. Without it the app cannot say anything about the sun, and it will not guess. |
| **Fixed** | Place things that are not tables: bar, kitchen, toilets, stage, fireplace, awning. They also tell diners what is near a table. |
| **Zones** | Name areas (window row, terrace, quiet corner). |
| **Tables** | Add tables (round or rectangular), set how many people they seat, which are accessible, which suit a high chair, which can be joined, and place the **chairs**. Select several tables to move, align or duplicate them together. |
| **Light** | Pick a date and a time to see where the sun falls in the room, when it gets dark, and what each table is like at that moment, as a diner will see it. |

You can have several rooms (for example **Main room** and **Terrace**); an outdoor room is marked as such. The editor warns you as
you draw: a table outside the walls, two tables overlapping, a window with no wall under it, windows but no north. Warnings do
not stop you saving a draft; errors stop you publishing.

**Tables with bookings cannot be deleted** (the editor says so): switch the table off instead, and it stops being offered.

## 6. Review and publish

The review page is a checklist. Each item says **Done** or **To do** in words, with a link that takes you to the step that fixes it:
name and cuisines, address and pin, opening hours with at least one service, at least one table, a room with no errors, and a north
for rooms with windows. A photo is only *recommended*. **Publish** switches on when every required item is done, and the server checks
everything again when you press it.

Once published, your restaurant appears in search, has its public page, and takes bookings. **Unpublish** hides it and stops new
bookings; bookings already made stay, and their manage links keep working.

## 7. After you publish

- Your **public page** shows your photos, hours, address with a map, contacts and the booking form.
- Diners can ask for a **sunny table**, **shade**, **a view**, a **quiet corner** or to be **away from the toilets**; the planner honours these
  when it can, and tells the diner when no such table is free.
- Each booking email carries your cover photo, your address, the table and chair, a few sentences about the light and the view, and a small
  map of the room with the booked table highlighted.
- Staff open the **dashboard** to run the floor (see [the staff guide](user-guide-staff.md)).

## If something looks wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Publish is greyed out | Read the **To do** items on the review page; each has a link to fix it |
| "No sun" everywhere | Set the north in the **North** tool and place the pin on the map |
| The map does not load | Type the latitude and longitude in the location step; the rest works without the map |
| "Someone saved a newer version" | Another tab saved first. Your work is kept; reload to see theirs, then re-apply what you need |
